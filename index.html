YOUR QUEST GUIDE

How to play

Guess the mystery word using the on-screen keyboard or your real keyboard. The squares tell you how close you are.

BRAVEGreen means B is correct and in the right position.
SMILEGold means M belongs somewhere else.
FROSTGray means F is not in the word.

Each grade receives a different daily word. Solve any one word to lock all ten grades until midnight Pacific Time. If you run out of attempts, you may try another grade. Results and the shared completion lock are stored in this browser, not in a school account.
